# male-anatomy.glb

Derived from **Z-Anatomy – The libre 3D atlas of anatomy**
(https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/The-blend), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Z-Anatomy is itself based on **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS),
https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html.

Changes made for Altsetter: only externally visible muscles, tendons and bones were kept,
muscles not trained in a gym (face, hands, feet, pelvic floor, …) were removed,
meshes were decimated, materials replaced, names cleaned up (".l"/".r" -> "left"/"right"),
and the result was exported as a Draco-compressed GLB.

This file (male-anatomy.glb) is therefore also licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
